## Release summary

Update from 0.1.0 to 0.2.0. Adds two exported functions for graphical
examination of exposure measurements on normal and log-normal probability
paper, with integration into the UNE-EN 689 Shiny application and report.
The graphical assessment does not automatically establish SEG validity.
Documentation and author metadata have also been updated.
