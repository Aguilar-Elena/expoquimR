# expoquimR 0.2.0

* Added `une689_probability_data()` and `une689_probability_plot()` for
  graphical examination of exposure distributions on probability paper.
* Integrated probability plots into the UNE-EN 689 Shiny application and
  the HTML report template.
* Expanded tests and documentation, and added a related research citation.
* Corrected package author metadata to Guillén Riquelme.

The new graphical output is an aid to professional interpretation, not an
automated determination of SEG validity.
